## Bonnie Dane Gilmore

When Bonnie Dane Gilmore was born on 17 June 1923, in Aquilla, Texas, her father, [Jesse Aubry Gilmore](/gilmore/_index), was 28 and her mother, [Clemmie Marie Scruggs](/gilmore/Clemmie-Marie-Scruggs), was 25. Her sister was Wanda Wayne Gilmore and her brother was Harlan Joe Gilmore. She had one daughter with Edward Charles Rees. She died on 1 December 2002, at the age of 79, and was buried in Restland Memorial Park, Dallas, Texas.
